This guide is dedicated to the signs on utility poles for electric power and communication lines in the city of Fujisawa.

Many areas of Fujisawa look very similar, while signs with the name of the area or an address are far from always present. At the same time, signs on utility poles can be found almost everywhere and often make it possible to identify an area or even a small part of it.

It should be noted that this guide probably does not cover all existing signs. In addition, some areas where a particular inscription may be found have been identified based on my own observations and may contain inaccuracies or errors.

[The boundaries of the areas can be viewed here](https://geoshape.ex.nii.ac.jp/ka/resource/14205.html)

### Inscriptions on the signs:

(Upper signs - NTT)

The lower signs sometimes contain historical place names that have survived from the time when the modern areas did not yet exist.

As a rule, the upper signs have one of the following suffixes: 支, 幹, 分, and sometimes 線 and 支線 - but there are exceptions for various reasons. The inscriptions may also have, presumably, full and abbreviated forms. The full form will generally be used, while the abbreviated form will also be noted where possible.

“Unidentified signs” are also found - these are signs that do not contain any company markings.

The lower signs may simply be gray and uncoated, like stainless steel. Newer signs painted white may have some decorative details.

Repeated inscriptions are marked with the symbol **~**  
The "?" symbol is used where it is not possible to identify a kanji.

**Area ratings:**  
**+++** - Worth learning first  
**++** - Can wait  
**—** - Questionable

**Rating criteria:**  
When assigning ratings, the main consideration was how difficult it is to determine the area without signs: how often blue signs or address stickers are found, as well as how easy it is to recognize the area from its overall appearance.

It was also taken into account how often the area appears in GeoGuessr when playing in this city and how much the utility pole signs narrow down the search.

The size of the area was also taken into account, but this criterion is less important.  
(All ratings are subjective)
